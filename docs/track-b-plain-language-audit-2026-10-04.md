# Track B: simpler screens, deeper nomination journey

4 October 2026. This revision supersedes the interface and copy in the [first persona implementation](persona-implementation-2026-10-04.md). It implements the user's request to audit verbosity and deepen Track B. It is a local prototype revision, not a new production deployment or a compliance certification.

## The story to demonstrate

Kavita helps Babulal organise one identifiable family account. They begin with a familiar paper, establish who services it and whether a nomination already exists, prepare the right institution request, understand its response, resolve a correction, and leave a reviewed sheet that their chosen person can use. Praveen can help locate the papers without receiving passwords or authority to sign or move money.

This is a fictional composite, not an observed interview or successful recovery. It connects the supplied personas around family continuity. Praveen's borrowed-capital trading and Kavita's scam exposure require separate protection; a nomination tracker cannot eliminate those risks. Brief safety guidance remains accessible as secondary help.

The depth claim is **one complete preparation, evidence and handover journey**. It does not mean automatic discovery, form submission, record verification, inheritance decisions or asset recovery. Track B values depth on one journey; the nomination tracker remains the chosen product, as recorded in [Track B](track-b.md) and the [build plan](build-plan.md).

## What the audit found and changed

| Finding | Revision | Practical effect |
|---|---|---|
| Home mixed the main task with several unfamiliar financial routes | One primary start action, familiar paper examples, secondary problems inside an expandable section | A newcomer has an obvious place to begin |
| Setup asked ownership, holding and product questions together | New-account context asks one question at a time; back and encrypted resume preserve that question | Less recall and fewer simultaneous decisions |
| Long rule paragraphs dominated preparation | Six short steps; provider detail, reasons, terms and sources remain expandable | Required action is visible before optional explanation |
| Regulatory guidance could look like a provider's form | Generic providers receive an ask-the-institution action; regulatory link stays in sources | A rule PDF is not presented as a usable nomination form |
| The nominee/holder distinction did not fully address inheritance confusion | Optional Asha/Tara practice adds an inheritance question with corrective feedback | Nomination cannot be mistaken for deciding legal inheritance |
| The user could lose lesson progress | Bounded checkpoints enter only deliberate encrypted saves, tied to the account context | A changed account invalidates an old lesson; practice never proves registration |
| A correction required retracing preparation | An unresolved request resumes directly at the reply/correction step | The next action addresses the actual barrier |
| The correction screen repeated all initial reply choices | Reply selection and correction reason are separate; user can return to the reply choice | One decision at a time |
| A follow-up letter lacked an explicit problem and requested fix | Optional short case description plus dated private events create a reviewable letter | Follow-up becomes concrete, without automatic filing |
| Nomination completion could obscure family readiness | Separate family review, paper location and three handover checks | A record is useful only if the chosen person can find and understand it |
| Copy changes could leave listening out of sync | Rebuild all current supplemental clips from exact committed public text and verify hashes | Listening follows the current wording; private entries are excluded |
| Draft translations contained obvious broken wording | Removed obsolete copy, repaired Odia/Dogri artifacts and Hindi/Urdu naming ambiguity | Structural checks improve the drafts; fluent approval remains necessary |

## The complete journey and its boundaries

1. **Identify one paper.** Alive/deceased is distinguished before preparation. Bank passbook/deposit receipt, electronic share statement and fund statement lead to the appropriate setup. Old physical share certificates lead to old-holding identification, without being assumed to be demat. Unknown account type has an institution question instead of a guessed route.
2. **Check the existing record.** Owner, sole/joint holding and deposit/fund context are explicit, with uncertainty choices. Fund units in demat use the demat nomination route. Bank requirements stay separate from securities requirements.
3. **Prepare privately.** Get the actual form/workflow, match its account reference, identify the nominee, establish whether child requirements apply, and review consent before sending through the institution. Full references, birth dates, documents, signatures and OTPs are not collected by Virasat. Branch/service-desk help is available.
4. **Interpret the reply.** Received, registered, correction/rejection, unclear and not sent are distinct. A received request remains awaiting confirmation. A correction remains unresolved. Uncertainty does not create evidence.
5. **Resolve a barrier.** Missing information, incorrect details and unclear reasons have different questions. Private notes preserve contact, correction, resubmission, escalation and replies. A short letter states the problem and requested fix. Downloading is not filing. Securities and bank grievance routes remain separate.
6. **Check the outcome.** Registration requires a dated institutional record and the user's explicit check. Status-only confirmation is distinct from checking nominee particulars. These are self-reports; the app does not authenticate the document or query the institution.
7. **Make the family ready.** Review what may be shared, identify the paper location, and check that the chosen person can find the sheet, explain the next step and obtain appropriate access without credentials on the sheet. Private case descriptions, replies and learning checkpoints stay out of family exports. A reminder date is a personal review habit, not a legal deadline.
8. **Handle a later death safely.** Stop new-nomination preparation and ask the institution's claim team for the applicable route and a written document list. Minor claimants have assistance and legal-aid orientation. This is claim preparation; no universal document checklist, entitlement decision or claim approval is asserted.

Securities particulars, child-date/guardian distinctions, joint consent and statement visibility were rechecked against the [SEBI circular dated 29 May 2026](https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/attachdocs/jun-2026/1780397706130.pdf), effective September 2026. Complaint orientation uses [SCORES](https://scores.sebi.gov.in/) for applicable securities complaints and [RBI complaint guidance](https://www.rbi.org.in/commonperson/English/Scripts/FAQs.aspx?Id=3407) for bank complaints. Institutions can change their workflows; users must obtain current instructions.

## What would establish real depth beyond the prototype

Run one supervised nomination case with the actual provider: obtain its current form, observe the user's completion, retain its acknowledgement, resolve any deficiency and examine its registration confirmation. Then give the reviewed family sheet to the chosen recipient and ask them to locate the papers and explain the next action without coaching. Keep the outcome, time, errors and assistance required; count failure and abandonment as results.

Review each public language with a fluent speaker and target-user listening session before calling it usable in that language. Test actual older phones, interrupted saves and a changing connection. Maintain provider instructions and regulatory sources with named owners and review dates. These are the remaining evidence gaps; adding more menus will not close them.
