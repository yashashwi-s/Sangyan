# Historical 15-audio published draft release review

The later 21-text/16-audio release and bounded startup fix are recorded in the [current release review](nepali-startup-release-2026-10-04.md). Evidence below remains tied to the earlier deployment.

Date: 4 October 2026. Virasat's release scope is **21 text languages: English plus 20 scheduled languages, excluding Bodo and Kashmiri**. Fifteen languages have complete bundled draft guidance recordings; Nepali, Konkani, Manipuri/Meitei, Sanskrit, Santali and Sindhi remain text-only. Structural completeness means 369 nonempty public dictionary strings per language and 357 validated public guidance clips per advertised audio language. It is not a fluent translation or pronunciation approval.

## What the final review checked

The full release gate passes **84 automated checks**. [The saved output](audits/2026-10-04-production/release-check.txt) records routing/schema/crypto validation, all effective dictionaries, all audio manifests and hashes, public-copy alignment, source/licence/review metadata, and playback/cache behavior. These tests run deterministic models and simulated browser APIs; their pass does not prove an actual mobile user's success.

The local preview originally omitted production CSP headers and served the `_headers` source file. This was a preview parity gap, not an observed production leak. The preview now derives the baseline policy from `app/vercel.json`, denies `_headers`, and passes the same public integrity/privacy verifier as production.

## Publication and live evidence

The deployment uses an isolated stage containing only `app/dist`, `app/vercel.json` and private project-link metadata outside the public output. `_headers` is omitted because Vercel uses `vercel.json`. Build-only translation sources, audio manifests/reports, Python/model dependencies, tests, research and audit traces are excluded. The [deployment input summary](audits/2026-10-04-production/deploy-inputs.json) records 5,424 uploaded public/configuration files; the supported compressed CLI archive was accepted without a size error.

The [live verifier](audits/2026-10-04-production/live-public-serving.json) compares all 21 native entry pages and dictionaries with local SHA-256; all 68 non-MP3 public files, including the shell, fonts, credits and generated catalogues; one entire `homeTitle` MP3 per audio language; and matching 64-byte `206` ranges. It checks locale/audio immutable caching, no cookies, no-referrer, nosniff, CSP/permissions, excluded Bodo/Kashmiri routes and 11 build-only/hosting paths returning 404. The remaining 5,340 MP3s have complete local validation and deployment packaging evidence; they are not described as individually downloaded and compared on the live origin.

Production address, deployment identifier, final source hash and verification time are recorded in `docs/audits/2026-10-04-production/deployment.json` after the final corrected deployment. The [final navigation report](bharat-performance-final-2026-10-04.md) distinguishes measured source versions and failed interrupted startups from successful fresh navigation.

## Remaining human evidence

This is a transparent draft release, not a 100% completion, GIGW/STQC/WCAG certification, fluent-language approval or physical 2 GB Android claim. The final CUA inventory returned no enabled apps/browsers; no substitute rendered-flow or listening observation is invented. Actual responsive/keyboard/screen-reader checks, fluent text and voice review, and independent target-user tasks remain necessary. Use one bank savings case, one deposit/joint case and one mutual-fund-in-demat case to observe identification, request-versus-registration understanding, interrupted return/save and a safe family handoff. Real-device tests need on-screen keyboards, many accounts, repeated outages, background termination and cache eviction. The selected government-source review and automated scores are evidence of design/checks, not government endorsement or measured ease of use.
