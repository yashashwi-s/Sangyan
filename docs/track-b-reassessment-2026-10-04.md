# Virasat: Track B reassessment and potential impact

4 October 2026. Strategic review of the current local prototype, the original brief, earlier research and fresh public evidence. The team confirms there are **no real-user trials or institutional outcomes**. Recommendations below are proposed work, not implemented features. The accompanying workbook contains an illustrative scenario, not a forecast calibrated from users.

## The decision

Keep the nomination and family-continuity direction. Build deeper around **one known securities account, one current institution workflow and one reviewed language**. The strongest outcome is that the holder understands the choice, completes the appropriate institution action, checks its result and leaves information a chosen person can actually use.

The present prototype demonstrates preparation, status recording, correction orientation and a family sheet. It does not yet establish that a first-time user can complete the institution's process, that the recorded status is correct, or that the recipient can use the sheet. That is the central remaining gap. More languages, recordings or screens do not establish those outcomes.

The original [problem statement](../references/problem-statement.pdf), pp. 3–6, supports this focus: depth on one complete journey, usable rights and protections, and a nominee tracker across demat, bank and fund accounts. Safety/impact and Bharat usability together carry 55% of the rubric. It does not require one product to eliminate every risk described by the three personas. Bank deposits remain a useful adjacent category, with separate rules; a securities case should anchor the Track B demonstration.

## The story and the three people

**Fictional illustration, not an interview:** Babulal finds an old fund statement. He does not know whether someone is named to make a claim. He chooses his language and starts from the paper. Virasat helps him distinguish a fund folio from fund units held in demat, identify the servicing institution and check its existing record. He decides what action he wants and obtains the current official process. If the institution asks for a correction, he records exactly what it requested, corrects it privately and keeps the reply. He checks the final nomination particulars. He then chooses what Kavita may see and where the underlying papers can be found. Kavita tries to locate them and explain the next step without coaching. Praveen can help navigate, with Babulal retaining control of signing and authentication.

The pressure comes from uncertainty, unfamiliar vocabulary, repeated requests, an intimidating external process and dependence on a helper. The resolution is a completed, understood action and a usable family handover. An old holding already transferred to IEPF follows a different recovery route. Naming a nominee today does not recover that holding or decide inheritance.

| Person and pain | What exists now | What remains unresolved / what to do |
|---|---|---|
| Babulal: does not know what exists or where old papers lead | Familiar-paper entry, account list, old-holding orientation | Known-account tracker cannot find forgotten accounts. Add a guided paper search and voluntary, direct referrals to CAS, MITRA, the named institution or IEPF according to context. Never imply a complete asset inventory. |
| Babulal: unfamiliar nomination, child/joint details and confusing replies | Six-step coach, uncertainty pauses, separate bank/securities guidance, receipt versus registration | Pilot a real current institution form, including one actual deficiency. Verify he can complete it with the allowed support and understands what the confirmation proves. |
| Babulal: cannot navigate IEPF/SCORES | Institution-first questions and links; dated private notes and follow-up draft | This is orientation. Build a precise complaint pack for the chosen nomination journey, with entity identity, request/reply dates, evidence locations and remedy requested. Add review-stage guidance after the ATR, with current eligibility checks. |
| Kavita: limited English and intimidation | 21 text packs, 16 listening languages, short steps and explanations on demand | Regional content is unreviewed. Audit one language from first screen through actual institution handoff. Include terminology, dates, errors, synthetic speech and spoken teach-back. |
| Kavita: Ponzi/fake-IPO exposure | Warning signs, pause practice and official reporting routes | No offer verification, payment interception or demonstrated fraud prevention. Keep this a clearly bounded secondary pathway. Test recognition of a fake offer and correct reporting choice separately. |
| Praveen: Telegram tips, urgency and borrowed trading capital | Brief safety guidance and practice | Cannot stop borrowing or loss-chasing. His main harm needs separate Track A/D/E interventions and behavioral evidence. Do not use trading losses as this tracker's recoverable opportunity. |
| All: dependence on helpers, shared devices and interruptions | Explicit encrypted saves, resumable drafts, public-only audio, optional sharing controls | Observe whether the user can save, locate and restore a file. A 12-character password and download workflow can fail despite sound encryption. Provide a usable paper route and a consented helper mode without sharing OTPs or signing authority. |
| Family recipient: does not know where to begin later | Separate family review and three handover checks | Current checks are the holder's self-report. Have the consenting recipient actually locate the sheet and papers, explain the next action and identify their own lawful institution-access route. |

The personas need not be related. The composite above is one illustration; recruitment and outcome reporting must preserve each persona's independent needs and consent.

### Current-rule checks

The [May 29, 2026 nomination circular](https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/attachdocs/jun-2026/1780397706130.pdf) takes effect September 1. It allows three nominees, keeps joint nomination optional, requires all joint holders' consent and supports online/offline routes. It distinguishes mandatory particulars from optional details and separates wet-signature from thumb-impression witnessing. The prototype broadly reflects these distinctions. This is a bounded rule review, not a compliance certification or proof of each provider's implementation. [The stale education page](https://investor.sebi.gov.in/market-nomination.html) must not be used for its old freezing warning.

## Quantifying potential impact honestly

Public data supports the problem's context. **Every funnel input below is an illustrative assumption**, independent of the public statistics. There is no public dataset here establishing the incidence of missing/wrong nominations, target-user uptake or the causal effect of this interface.

Define one cohort of **1,000 distinct known securities-account nomination cases**, from adult individual holders choosing to add/change nomination, in one reviewed provider/language route. Count every enrolled case over 90 days, including non-starts and abandonment. This is a planning cohort, not an observed pilot, unique people, an annual adoption forecast or the entire investor base. Existing correct nominations, opt-outs, bank cases and recovery cases are outside this particular funnel and have separate appropriate-action metrics.

| Assumption | Illustration | Denominator |
|---|---:|---|
| Confirmation through usual institution help | 40% | All eligible cases |
| Start Virasat task | 90% | All eligible cases |
| Reach correct institution process | 90% | Started cases |
| Submit appropriate nomination request | 85% | Correctly routed cases |
| Obtain confirmation matching intended particulars | 90% | Submitted cases |
| Recipient completes handover task, usual / Virasat | 40% / 75% | Confirmed cases in each arm |
| Mean active effort, usual / Virasat | 90 / 60 minutes | All eligible cases in each arm, including unsuccessful attempts |

The causal chain is:

`1,000 eligible → 900 start → 810 correct route → 688.5 submitted → 619.65 confirmed → 464.7375 confirmed plus family-ready`

Fractional values are expected scenario counts, not fractional observed people. Use unrounded values in calculations and rounded values for presentation.

| Outcome | Usual help | Virasat scenario | Additional scenario outcome |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Confirmed nominations with intended particulars | 400 | about 620 | about 220 |
| Confirmed account records usable by the chosen recipient | 160 | about 465 | about 305 |
| Total active effort | 1,500 hours | 1,000 hours | 500 hours less |

The 305 family-ready records overlap confirmed nominations. Do not add the two counts, call them 305 unique families, or multiply either by portfolio value. Time assumptions are all-case averages; simply timing successful cases would introduce selection bias. Calendar processing time, user active effort and helper effort need separate records.

**Sensitivity matters:** keeping other inputs fixed, the conditional confirmation rate after submission must exceed about 58.1% to outperform the assumed 40% usual-help rate. At 50%, the model produces 344.25 confirmations, **55.75 fewer** than usual help. If active effort is 100 minutes instead of 60, the model adds about166.7 hours. Negative impact remains visible; it is not clamped to zero. These are scenarios, not confidence intervals.

The [editable workbook](../outputs/01a105c0-a1b4-78e2-a0b0-36c05040429f/virasat-impact-model.xlsx) keeps results, inputs and calculation steps together and public observations on a separate evidence tab. Blank/invalid inputs make dependent results unavailable; real zero remains zero. Money recovered, losses prevented and measured Virasat outcomes remain **not measured**, not ₹0 and not an invented positive value. The workbook was recalculated, exported and reimported; ten documented checks cover missing/invalid inputs, genuine zero, negative effects, public-table reconciliation and retained formulas. Both tabs were rendered and visually checked. [Validation record](audits/2026-10-04-reassessment/workbook-validation.json). Native Excel/LibreOffice was not exercised.

## How to replace assumptions with evidence

First conduct a proposed 12–18-person formative study across the three personas, including low literacy, older-phone/shared-device use and a recipient task. It diagnoses comprehension and completion failures; it cannot establish population-level effectiveness. Use fake records for practice and no sensitive data in analytics.

Then run a prospective comparison of Virasat against **normal current institution help**, with the same route, language, task and 90-day outcome window. Assign at holder/household level to avoid contamination across their accounts; report account outcomes with household clustering. Separate assisted and unassisted delivery and record helper time. A 40–60-person feasibility pilot is a proposed starting point for recruitment/follow-up, not a powered impact study. Determine a confirmatory sample after baseline completion, expected effect, clustering and attrition are known. Report denominators and uncertainty, including language/provider segments.

| Measure | Evidence and denominator |
|---|---|
| Appropriate action resolved | All assigned eligible cases; includes independently checked correct nomination, correction or informed appropriate opt-out in the wider study. Nomination-only model remains a separate cohort. |
| Confirmed intended nomination | All nomination-seeking assigned cases; dated institutional record checked privately with consent against intended particulars. Self-report-only and status-only cases reported separately. |
| First submission accepted without documentary rework | All submitted eligible requests; no claim that an acknowledgement alone proves completeness. |
| Family usability | All assigned cases and, separately, confirmed cases; consenting recipient finds papers and explains correct next action without coaching. Do not condition the primary causal comparison only on successful completers. |
| Comprehension retained | Same short scenario tasks immediately and 7–30 days later; holder versus nominee, succession distinction, receipt versus registration and credential safety. Record prompting. |
| Active time and direct expense | All attempts, failures and helper sessions; actual travel/printing costs. Keep elapsed institution time separate. Monetize only documented costs or an explicitly chosen time valuation. |
| Redress outcome | Filed complaint reference, ATR receipt/review dates, institution action and claimant acceptance separately. Disposal is not equivalent to satisfactory resolution. |
| Harm and autonomy | Wrong route, coercive nomination choice, credential exposure, privacy incidents, false reassurance, recovery failure and abandonment. |
| Delivery feasibility | Real device/network, complete task bytes including audio/retries, support minutes, source/language-review work and operating cost. |

For privacy-preserving outcome verification, collect random study IDs and minimum stage metadata with consent. Inspect documents locally or under the institution's agreed protocol; retain no central full account references, passwords, OTPs or unredacted records by default. No institutional partnership, participant consent or verification access is presumed. Without verification access, report self-reported outcomes separately and retain the evidence gap.

## What to do next, in order

1. Choose one securities provider/form and one fluent-reviewed language. Close submission, correction and result checking for that exact route; preserve uncertainty for unsupported cases.
2. Make the family recipient's actual task and encrypted/paper recovery part of that same journey. These are decisive safety outcomes.
3. Strengthen the nomination complaint pack and post-reply guidance, then correct the shallow MITRA/UDGAM referrals. Keep recovery orientation separate from the core outcome.
4. Run formative comprehension/device sessions, then the prospective institution-outcome comparison. Replace workbook assumptions with observations and a supported counterfactual.
5. Establish source-review ownership, voice rights, distribution cost and assisted-service capacity before widening language/provider coverage.

Retain the short screens, progressive disclosure, private-by-default records and deterministic distinctions already implemented. Avoid returning to a long learning curriculum, an all-asset discovery promise or a will/tax engine to create an appearance of depth.

## What can be presented now

“Virasat helps a first-time investor work through a nomination task in familiar language, distinguish a receipt from registration, follow up on a correction, and prepare a reviewed family account sheet.” Demonstrate the fictional case and label all institution replies as examples. Show the private-data boundary and one honest uncertainty branch.

Present public problem statistics with their periods and definitions. Present the 1,000-case model only as **potential impact under stated assumptions**, including its downside. Current technical proof includes 155 passing release checks and bounded browser, privacy, language, delivery and workspace evidence in the [plain-language audit](track-b-plain-language-audit-2026-10-04.md). Those measurements are reused, not rerun in this strategic review. The fresh observation confirmed the current English home; current implementation modules were reread. No fresh whole-flow user trial, physical-phone result, certification or production deployment is claimed.

A public live demo still needs to match the intended current local release. The brief's 3–5-minute scenario video and PPT were not found among workspace deliverables in this review. Prepare those around the completed bounded journey and the evidence limits; this research review itself does not create them.
