## One system, several connected investigations

The shared object is **a securities evidence dossier**. It can contain:

1. The security's history: identity, corporate actions and quantities.
2. The account/folio history: where it was held and how it moved.
3. The person's supported role: holder, survivor, nominee or claimant.
4. The claim route: current rule, required evidence and outstanding questions.
5. The institution's responses: deficiencies, acknowledgements and eventual credit.
6. The surviving financial history: supported acquisition facts for future use.

This makes demat history and inheritance coherent. It also avoids turning the project into unrelated “asset discovery + chatbot + will maker + tax app” modules.

## Strongest complete journey to validate

**An adult child helps a family member prepare an uncontested claim for an old shareholding that has undergone a corporate action.** They have several inconsistent records. The system reconstructs what those records support, identifies the applicable current route, pinpoints missing evidence, and exports a clear dossier. When a credit confirmation is provided, it reconciles the result and preserves the acquisition history.

That is a candidate journey, not a claim that all the evidence needed is already in hand. The legal route should be chosen only after a real or carefully synthetic case has been reviewed.

## Research areas ranked by value to that journey

| Research area | Why it belongs | What a successful investigation produces |
|---|---|---|
| Historical security/holder matching | Old documents rarely use today's terminology consistently | Verified aliases and an event chain, with uncertain matches exposed |
| Current transmission rules | The family needs the correct procedural route | Versioned, reviewed decision conditions tied to the case |
| Evidence consistency | A form can be syntactically complete but substantively contradictory | Page-level mismatch report and precise corrective evidence request |
| Quantity and cost reconstruction | Corporate actions and movements affect what should arrive and what history remains | Deterministic calculations with a derivation trail |
| Deficiency and acknowledgement semantics | The procedural clock depends on what the institution received/accepted | Separate submission, completeness, response and credit dates |
| Target-user comprehension | A technically correct dossier can still be unusable | Observed completion/error rates for the intended users |

## What to reserve for later

Universal automatic account discovery, disputed estates, multi-jurisdiction succession, all physical issuers, complete tax return preparation, live institutional submissions and automated grievance filing. These depend on additional authority, data and legal review.
